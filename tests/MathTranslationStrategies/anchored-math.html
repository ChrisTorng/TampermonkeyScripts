Formula position: isolate SVG images from Edge translation

The earlier image approach keeps complete formulas but Android Edge 155 changed T0 M0 T1 M1 T2 M2 T3 into T0 M0 M1 T1 T2 M2 T3. This test anchors formula images to their preceding prose in several different ways. The purpose is to detect actual ordering, not just count images.
