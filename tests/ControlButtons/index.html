Fullscreen

Scroll down to this canvas and note the page position. Drag the dim gray fullscreen icon with a mouse or finger: releasing must not enter fullscreen. Click to enter: the button disappears and no close button is shown. Press Esc to exit: the page and any game scrollbars must return to their pre-entry positions. Browser Back also restores the expanded fallback. Reload to reset the button position.
